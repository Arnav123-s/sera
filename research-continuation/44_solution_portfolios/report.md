## Examples generated from retained knowledge

For linearly changing acceleration, one proposed mass route is:

`m = 2*f*t / (a1*t^2 + 2*v - 2*v0)`.

It uses force, elapsed time, acceleration slope and two velocities, retaining the nonzero-denominator condition. Another proposed route infers time from `a0`, `a1`, force and mass:

`t = (f - a0*m) / (a1*m)`.
