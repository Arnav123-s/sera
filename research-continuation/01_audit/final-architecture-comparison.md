# Architecture comparison after the continuation

I compared the implementation with the original architecture handbook, both delivered research bundles, and the adopted generative-geometry handoff after running the experiments. The work follows the recommended V2-B direction, but it is **not the complete architecture**. The central unresolved requirements are learned applicability, a reusable compiler connected to the current learner, shared positive transfer, continuous latent migration, and independently improved investigation.

The original handbook's R1/R2 paths and 154 source concepts remain covered by the historical [shared source audit](../../reports/shared-source-audit.md). ## Comparison with the packet's models

The first packet's state-aware associative network is not replaced by the circle fit. It remains a candidate memory mechanism whose 57 checkpoints and 603 conditions were reproduced. The SERA 0.5 shared recurrent owner remains the operational parent. Its separate R2 instruments and finite controller stay explicit inherited components.

The second packet's Bayesian numeric world model uses supplied features and controlled probes. GG-P0 and GG-ACT add finite alternatives for the same observed trajectory, symbolic/program controls, a noisy nuisance action, and explicit omitted-class failures. These studies still receive the coordinate and grammar. They cannot demonstrate discovery of a circle from unordered points or spontaneous acquisition of a physical law.

`GenerativeSharedR1` moves a four-class posterior into the registered R1 owner and proves persistence, corrections and conditional retention. It does **not** couple that posterior to the recurrent fusion computation. This matters: common ownership is a necessary integration property, while positive transfer is a separate empirical claim. The standalone inquiry study retains an explicit analytic side state; I do not describe its 300 runs as integrated neural training.

Kavi contributes owned executable definitions, complete dependency identities and invalidation semantics. No phase vector is relabeled as a SERA latent vector, and no upstream Kavi fixes are claimed merged. Its source-audit aliasing and identity findings are addressed in the new SERA contracts, while the pinned upstream remains preserved.

## Corrections to overbroad interpretations

The geometry protocol's original floating-storage label, initialization-seed wording and soft budget claim have explicit errata. The original frozen source and all results remain recoverable. The first Windows memory observation measured a launcher, not the complete process tree; the corrected job measurement is labeled committed memory, never RSS. Independent algebra exposed and repaired two new precision defects without weakening test tolerances.

The strongest new negative result is repeated across the experiments: fitting a compact model and assigning it high posterior weight does not establish its domain of validity. Local residual repair even worsened unseen-arc coverage in GG-P0. In GG-ACT the omitted-pattern family retained high class confidence and poor coverage. These findings block a broad understanding or reliable-applicability claim.

M1 remains restricted mechanism evidence. M2 is open. M3 and M4 are not established. The next step is the [guard and consolidation protocol](../NEXT_EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL.md), followed by the packet's investigator training and crossed-generation gates.
