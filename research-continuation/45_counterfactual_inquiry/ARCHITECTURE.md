# One owner, deeper investigations

`InquirySession` extends the current `ReconciledSession` through the same `CreditR1` object. `CounterfactualR1` adds a procedure-value head, compatible-world weights and compact normalized-response coefficients. Earlier language, reading, memory, operators, physical meanings, empirical fits and rational solutions remain registered on the same object. The integration audit compares every inherited tensor and earlier practical task output.

The investigation has seven connected operations:

1. Inspect retained executable dependencies and admitted word meanings. Generate prospective variable questions from that inventory, balancing unexplored domains and suppressing completed identities.
2. Execute learned forward and inverse coefficients under alternative declared control mechanisms. Commit every proposed world before receiving evidence. Equivalent predictions keep their alternative assumptions.
3. Describe consequences: increasing/decreasing responses, stationary points, zeros, singularities and guarded limits. A condition belongs to its branch and survives algebraic simplification.
4. Choose a discriminating observation. Begin with the original target; investigate another observable when the answer agrees but its explanations remain different.
5. Update compatible-world weights from independently generated measurements with explicit error bounds. An empty set triggers a broader mechanism basis and a return to the same original question. The qualified successor also challenges an apparently successful narrow consensus against the full retained basis before completion; this correction and its predecessor are preserved separately.
6. Check the completed trajectory and propose background-independent response ratios. A separate structural implementation proves the ratio symbolically and checks it on fresh backgrounds.
7. Store guarded reusable coefficients, bind credit to the original goal/predictor/source/proof, and attach conventional terminology after verification. Move to another unexplored dependency rather than accumulating points from repetitions.

## What changes through learning

The existing operator and inverse weights construct each imagined world. New observed evidence changes compatible-world coefficients. Verified practice updates the eight-feature procedure head through ridge sufficient statistics. Independently checked compact response coefficients become retained executable weights. The record layer stores lineage, scope, provenance, unfinished goals and proofs around those computations.

The question enumeration, mechanism grammar, probe candidates, checker and point rule are engineered. The represented domains and simulator family are inherited or supplied. The comparison measures which selector works on the frozen tasks; its result determines deployment. Shared ownership is verified directly and does not substitute for a measured transfer result.

## Compact generators and precise scope

For a proposed mechanism with controls b, intervention multiplier z and learned response y(b,z), the learner tests whether y(b,z)/y(b,1) is independent of b. A surviving ratio r(z) yields the compact generator y(b,z)=y(b,1)r(z). The checker independently verifies y(b,z)-y(b,1)r(z)=0. The retained contract requires a nonzero initial target and the original guards in both worlds.

This provides a reusable relation across contexts, instead of storing a large list of examples. Mechanism assumptions remain explicit: changing mass while controlling force and changing mass while controlling acceleration define different investigations. Predicted worlds, simulated observations and exact conditional identities have separate record types. Later explanatory names describe an already checked relationship and do not leak a target equation into its proposal.

## Preservation and resumption

The old owner stays in its original store. The original experiment uses append-only revisions in `runs/sera-counterfactual-live`; its completion-challenge successor uses `runs/sera-counterfactual-qualified-live`. Every study question retains initial proposals, chosen decisions, observations, expansions, assessments and replay receipts. A pending decision is written before measurement, so resumption repeats its exact deterministic source query rather than inventing another outcome. Completed study phases refuse reruns. Public restoration checks source and artifact hashes and rejects divergent local files.
