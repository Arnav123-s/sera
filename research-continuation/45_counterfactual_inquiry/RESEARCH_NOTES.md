A familiar equation or repeated successful answer does not create new discovery credit.

The user's follow-up discussion is consistent with the implemented Stage 44 question grammar. [Pearl's causal-inference overview](https://ftp.cs.ucla.edu/pub/stat_ser/r350.pdf), especially section 3.4, motivates separating an intervention from merely conditioning on an observed variable. A counterfactual changes a declared structural assignment while retaining its background context. Alternative assignments therefore carry separate assumptions; algebra alone does not establish which assignment describes an observed system.

[Hauser and Bühlmann](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.4174v3) compare targeted and random interventions under explicit causal-model assumptions. This motivates matched probe-selection controls here. Their graphical-identification guarantees are not applied to SERA's different program family. Expected discrimination, numerical spread, balanced coverage and learned verified-progress selection must earn their admission through prospective comparison.

The model receives no new explanatory text or named target law while forming a conjecture. Independent checking follows a durable commitment. Only then may a verified relationship receive conventional terminology and attributed explanatory material. Simulated evidence stays identified as simulated; structural hypotheses, model-conditional limits and observed facts occupy separate records.

The continuing agenda should respond to an exhausted or contradicted representation by expanding the available mechanism grammar using retained operators and retrying its original question. A saturation record prevents cycling through equivalent requests. Broader investigation is measured by new explanatory coverage, successful discriminating experiments, transfer and retained behavior, rather than question count or points alone.
