# Why early agreement was insufficient

The original validation completed all 31 questions and five selectors. All alternatives had exact conditional proofs; those proofs certified the consequences of their stated mechanisms. The failure was in deciding that the remaining narrow-family mechanism sufficiently explained the observations.

One concrete case asked, “What changes in position when mass changes, under different compatible mechanisms?” At multiplier 1/16, the simulated position was approximately -50.0794 with error bound 0.00851. That one observation left one initial candidate. The loop closed the question even though its larger retained basis could construct another mechanism agreeing with that measurement and differing elsewhere. The old selector then gave eight wrong exact-consensus answers at the reserved validation multipliers.

The original result is preserved at `runs/CI-study-001/validation/406b779c74b2ddc25b984c43bec8e016a52797b732fc4e697d4f9726a67dac0c/entropy.json`. The same failure appeared under the learned selector on a separate question. Both cases are targeted regression fixtures; they belong to development, not the untouched final partition.

The repair changes completion. An apparently adequate explanation is challenged against the complete retained basis before it is accepted. Existing measurements constrain the enlarged portfolio. Remaining measurement attempts are selected to separate the new alternatives; when the budget ends, unresolved alternatives remain in the answer. The correction does not award correctness for confidence or turn a model-specific identity into an observed event.

The first two targeted repair checks retained every original observation, introduced no source hint, stayed within ten measurement attempts and removed the wrong consensus while covering the reference outcomes. Independent fresh replay matched the repaired trajectories exactly. The full repaired study has a separate source seal, owner store and records. Both the original validation failure and its complete costs remain part of the project evidence.

The original code, 26-question practice, 31-question validation and experimental owner remain available. The successor attaches a new procedure head to that actual owner and revises its evidence-compatible belief weights. Original learned mathematics, language, physical meanings and earlier checkpoints are preserved.
