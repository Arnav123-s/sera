# Results: grounded instructions and on-request reacquisition

I qualified a narrow learned mathematical-language interface and integrated it into the persistent workbench. The same owner now supports task-time changes to language parameters while preserving its previous neural core, numerical contexts, continuing motion state, factual history and finite programs. All failed and unpromoted variants remain available.

## What was taught

The learner receives English instructions for either `a*x-b=c (mod 11)` or `a*(x-b)=c (mod 11)`, with coefficients zero to ten expressed as digits or English number words. It predicts four slots: operation order and the three coefficients. Arithmetic execution and independent exhaustive substitution are supplied mechanisms; the network is learning the mapping from wording to a formal statement.

The new interface has 30,243 parameters: lexical and position embeddings, normalization, four attention queries and four output heads. Every word passes through the inherited width-256 R1 recurrent memory and fusion. The retained owner is the project's eight-head, dimension-32 delta-memory condition. This is not the complete rotor/density reference configuration. The original reference implementations and their evidence are preserved.

I used 4,096 teaching sentences, 256 development sentences, 600 optimizer steps and batches of 32 per model. Three seeds were paired across unchanged-core interface learning, shared-core adaptation, and a reinitialized-core control: nine models and 172,800 teaching draws. The adapted-core conditions expose 435,508 trainable parameters. Equal optimizer-step counts do not mean equal parameter counts or identical compute costs.

Training, development and final cases have disjoint coefficient triples. Of four sentence templates per operation order, three are taught initially and one is reserved as a new-structure challenge. Each final bank has 512 sentences per seed; repeated semantics and wording variants are reported and do not constitute independent learners. Selection uses development loss; final outcomes never alter the frozen training configuration or the preselected deployment seed.

## Learning missing wording

Each model receives two separate lessons from its saved predecessor. The new-word lesson teaches the preallocated but initially unadmitted token `scale` using 48 labeled examples. Its update is restricted to one 256-coordinate embedding. This does not demonstrate open vocabulary expansion. The new-structure lesson uses 192 examples and updates the language interface. Both use 240 steps, separate development checks, and 512 final examples. The curriculum and optimizer are supplied, not learned eta.

For the three unchanged-core models, mean new-word translation rises from 78.13% to 99.93%. New-structure translation reaches 99.41%. The latter also reduces performance on earlier sentence families to 37.30% on average. I retain that forgetting explicitly; it cannot be described as universal capability growth.

The empirical wording guard is imperfect. After the structure lesson it accepts 1,275 of 1,536 cases and admits five mistranslations, concentrated in the weaker seed. Formal arithmetic checks still pass for these wrong interpretations. I preserve this failure and do not promote a statistical semantic-risk guarantee.

I therefore add an independent, supplied finite-language contract to the application. A neural interpretation must match that contract before its answer can be returned. The checker passes exhaustive coefficient/template tests and rejects all five archived wrong admissions, retaining 1,270 correct answers: 82.68% coverage and zero accepted errors in that corrective check. This is validation on preserved outcomes, not a fresh accuracy experiment. It does not make the network more accurate or supply general English semantics.

## One learner over six successive requests

The separate prospective continuing run keeps one actual parameter owner and restores it after every request. Its schedule requests familiar wording, a new word, a new construction, earlier wording again, the new construction again, and the learned word in an earlier form.

All six requested answers are independently correct. Five requests trigger actual learning: 816 teaching examples and 1,200 optimizer steps in total. The full supervised job costs 118.47 seconds. All seven revisions, the previous owner states, optimizer/RNG checkpoints and teaching examples remain preserved.

New learning visibly withdraws earlier forms. The next request for such a form retrains it rather than loading an old answer or silently reverting to a predecessor. Fresh cases of the requested shapes score 99.22–100%; 1,021 of 1,023 such cases are translated exactly. The broader six 512-case family banks contain 3,064 correct translations out of 3,072. These are finite evaluations of one continuing learner, not independent trials of a general researcher.

The later output contract also accepts all six recorded requested answers. Earlier raw prediction errors stay in the evidence. Three original numerical contexts and all 85 interaction observations are preserved throughout the continuing run.

## Integration and verification

The application persists language tensors with checksummed references and records their interpreter identity. Every existing route still refers to the same R1 object. After owner changes, original factual observations are replayed and finite skills are re-proved. The frozen A08 serialization projection excludes only the added language interface; the complete owner identity and language checkpoint are saved separately.

Accepted interpretations now execute the previously acquired guarded affine program, with the second operation order compiled as `a*x-(a*b)=c`. Its answers must agree with the independent arithmetic path. Degenerate zero-coefficient cases are checked too. This execution change has an explicit saved-state migration: both the complete owner hash and language checkpoint remain unchanged, and the earlier runtime sources are archived. A browser request after migration returns `x = 5` by this route without another lesson; reloading the page preserves that result. This is executable program reuse, not a claim of positive neural transfer.

An integration test restores this owner, adds a new data task, continues a motion action, reuses the finite solver, reloads the result and rejects a tampered language checkpoint. The full 241-test suite passes in one run. After the corrective contract was added, its two new tests and the complete application integration test also pass. There are 243 distinct test cases across these runs; this is not a claim of a single 243-test full run. Exact interrupted-lesson resumption was separately checked against uninterrupted learning.

## Remaining research

This release establishes a limited task-triggered language-acquisition and reacquisition loop. It does not learn arbitrary English from documents, search and understand scientific literature, invent a prerequisite graph, discover the arithmetic verifier, or construct new general mathematical proofs. Its lesson selector and optimizer remain engineered. Accumulating these capabilities is distinct from independently improving the procedure that acquires them.

The next discriminating experiment should test acquisition from external grounded material and unfamiliar structures beyond the supplied templates, with a separate verifier, retrieval provenance, maintenance/reacquisition costs and a matched fresh-learner control. The larger Riemann-hypothesis research example remains a target for that missing capability stack, not a result of this release.
