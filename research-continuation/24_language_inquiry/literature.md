# Mechanism choices and research boundary

I read the v5 brief, start/status/evidence ledgers, report and cookbook, particularly A03–A10, and revisited three primary publication pages on 2026-09-16. The external review depth here is the authors' abstracts and publication metadata; this cycle does not reproduce those systems or claim a full-paper review.

[LAPS, Wong et al. (2021)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/wong21a.html) uses language to guide program-library learning and search. That is a stronger goal than a parser feeding a fixed executor. This integration establishes an owned executable interface first; its supplied composer and fixed inquiry scheduler do not acquire abstractions or search procedures. Learned program construction remains a competing next mechanism.

[COGS, Kim and Linzen (2020)](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.731/) separates familiar parsing from generalization to new combinations of known words and structures. I therefore report action compositions, surface patterns and trained spans separately. SERA's new interface receives sentence decomposition and entity binding; perfect results within that contract do not establish grammar discovery. These synthetic controls are not a COGS benchmark run.

[Memoised Wake-Sleep, Hewitt et al. (2020)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v124/hewitt20a.html) retains discovered programs while learning generative models and inference. Retaining unfinished investigations and valid exact prefixes addresses only persistence and duplicate computation here. It does not implement wake-sleep or turn a cache into learned abstraction.

The local competing controls are a learned bag-of-words span decoder with the same new parameter count and supervision, the learned recurrent span decoder using actual SERA R1, supplied formalization, and language-ignored zero controls. V5's independently saved whole-utterance GRU and grammar-assisted parser provide additional evidence about architecture choices; their outcomes are not additional seeds for this new experiment.

The original architecture's one-owner, evidence-kind, dependency and separate state/parameter/procedure contracts are retained. Online interaction parameters and the new language interface change under their declared learning rules. The procedure remains engineered. GG-GUARD-002 is a separate, newer valid local result with a restricted calibration contract; this new language-to-control route makes no inherited acceptance guarantee.
