## Decision and next research dependency

The original [architecture contract](architecture-audit.md) remains the guide: executable meaning, evidence-aware updates and resumable work are connected here, while grammar discovery, broader-state adequacy, learned hypothesis construction, informative action selection, retained shared-representation plasticity and improved acquisition procedures remain unresolved.

It must use a new calibration contract and untouched world seeds, rather than retuning against this exposed final cohort.
