## Where this goes beyond the previous cycle

The earlier transfer result predicted one future position with a protected readout. This cycle selects actions that change future motion, corrects a changing action response from observations, and measures the decision value of imagined consequences. It does not merely share a serialization owner with an independent specialist: the online sufficient statistics are registered in the actual shared R1, and every retained interface still points to that owner. This remains analytic workspace learning rather than a learned shared neural representation.

The gate establishes a scoped A08 control component, not the complete internal-world objective. The cost advantage survives the prespecified work-price proxy, while controller/runtime cost remains above the reactive baseline. There is no claim of superiority at exactly equal machine computation.

The strongest new failure is uncertainty undercoverage. A high-utility controller can still have an incomplete uncertainty model. Earlier independent-world certificates cover different frozen query policies and cannot repair this by being attached to the new runtime. Further uncertainty qualification must use a complete specified adaptive policy and fresh independent worlds; the completed A08 outcomes are now research evidence, not reusable final holdouts.
