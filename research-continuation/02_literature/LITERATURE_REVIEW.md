Prepared and checked **2026-09-14**. Evidence class **F — LITERATURE-SUPPORTED** for paper mechanisms; architectural imports and protocols remain **G — PROPOSED**. Mathematical deductions are labelled separately. No external paper's results were independently reproduced by this literature subtask.

## 1. Compression must be predictive, conditional and fully charged

The circle hypothesis is scientifically useful as *structured generation*: a finite relation and parameters can produce arbitrarily many consequences at a chosen numerical precision. MDL supplies the model/parameter/data tradeoff; information bottleneck makes clear that relevance depends on the future variable or task being preserved. Neither provides a finite universal container for arbitrary independent facts. [Rissanen](https://research.ibm.com/publications/modeling-by-shortest-data-description), [Tishby, Pereira and Bialek](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0004057).

**E — MATHEMATICALLY-DERIVED:** a fixed B-bit memory plus a fixed decoder has at most 2^B encodings. Exact recovery of every arbitrary N-bit input requires an injective map from 2^N messages, hence B >= N. Fixed decoder cost can be shared only when that decoder is actually shared and disclosed. Additional precision, a selected program, an input index, an external grammar, a residual store or a hidden seed is information and must be charged. This is a counting argument, not a measured SERA result.

**G — PROPOSED:** report actual artifact bytes and a prediction-risk frontier, plus any theoretical code-length calculation under its stated coding convention. Count generator, parameters, precision, decoder, applicability, uncertainty, exceptions, residuals, dependencies, index and externally supplied grammar. Report standalone and explicitly amortized sizes. Total acquisition computation includes model search and failed candidates. A model with three coefficients can lose the total-cost comparison for a small dataset if its decoder and guards are large.

A purported sufficient memory K is relative to a query/context distribution. Empirical accuracy on one query family does not establish H(X|K,C)=0 for all future questions. Unknown structure requires alternative predictions or abstention. The first useful question is therefore which future queries can be answered reliably after serialization, not how many mathematical points can be drawn.

## 2. Uncertainty over parameters is different from uncertainty over the class

An ellipse fitter can be very certain about the best ellipse even when the world contains a spiral. Bayesian model averaging provides a formal predictive mixture over candidate classes and their parameters. Model identification research separately treats reducing a class's parameter uncertainty and discriminating alternative mechanisms. Both motivate a belief over (M,theta), plus a check that the menu itself may be inadequate. [Hoeting et al.](https://www.stat.colostate.edu/~jah/papers/statsci.pdf), [Liu, Maini and Baker](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.11311).

**G — PROPOSED:** start with a finite menu and a common observation model. Preserve all candidates consistent with the data or a coherent posterior over them. Do not convert residual-plus-complexity scores into advertised probabilities without a probabilistic definition and calibration. A normalized posterior over a closed menu cannot express an omitted mechanism automatically; add held-out predictive checks and an explicit reopen/reject route.

Three noncollinear observations determine a circle only inside the circle family. For an unordered point cloud, distance to a fitted curve is not by itself a normalized point-generation likelihood. For t-to-coordinate prediction, the phase coordinate and trigonometric primitives are already inductive bias. These two experiments must remain distinct.

A neural representation adds no automatic identifiability guarantee. Locatello et al. show that the unsupervised disentanglement problem is generally underdetermined without appropriate biases, and their studied disentanglement metrics did not automatically yield downstream sample-efficiency gains. This is negative evidence against interpreting a visually tidy latent as recovered mechanism, not an argument that useful representation learning is impossible. [Locatello et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/locatello19a/locatello19a.pdf).

Calibration also has a sampling contract. Standard split conformal reasoning relies on exchangeability; an actively selected continual stream can violate that contract. The reviewed extensions provide qualified robustness, not unrestricted guarantees under any shift. Use independent calibration episodes and explicitly test shifted regimes. [Barber et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2202.13415).

## 3. Representation competition needs supplied-bias controls

AI Feynman, PySR and SINDy offer progressively different ways to search within a supplied language: expression decomposition, evolutionary symbolic search and sparse coefficients in a candidate dynamical library. These are strong controls for different stages; they do not erase the cost or bias of the primitives. [AI Feynman 2.0](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.10782), [PySR v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.01582v3), [SINDy](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.03580).

**G — PROPOSED:** first compare a bounded grammar against a polynomial/Fourier library and a neural predictor. Mark which constants, expressions and classes were learned. Evaluate omitted input regions, changed parameters and noise, plus a deliberately wrong grammar. For dynamics, use unseen initial states and forcing; fitting an observed trajectory is weaker than acquiring an intervention-valid evolution rule. A basis that works in convenient coordinates must also be challenged with coordinate changes.

Other geometry should enter only where its algebra suits the task. Hyperbolic embeddings give a reason to compare tree-like relations against Euclidean embeddings, but an explicit graph or small ancestor program is a strong baseline. Complex rotations give useful relational inverses/composition and periodic features, but are not a universal representation. [Poincare embeddings](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.08039), [RotatE](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.10197).

**E — MATHEMATICALLY-DERIVED:** componentwise complex rotations commute, so they cannot in general distinguish AB from BA for noncommuting target operations. A complex linear map B+iC is equivalent to the real block map [[B,-C],[C,B]]. Thus exact realification is a mandatory control for a claimed intrinsic advantage of complex arithmetic. Numerical precision and scalar state counts must be matched. The relevant contrast is the chosen operator class and implementation, not a quantum label.

The eight requested controls remain: raw episodes, kNN/interpolation, fixed circle, model-family selection, symbolic regression, neural latent, programmatic generator and generator-plus-residual memory. The ten requested tasks remain: circles, ellipses, periodic functions, spirals, piecewise curves, hierarchical relations, order-sensitive transforms, dynamics, random labels and systematic near-circle exceptions. [The protocol](GEN_GEOMETRY_PROTOCOL_RECOMMENDATION.md) stages these explicitly; the first four-task experiment does not stand in for completed coverage.

## 4. Investigation should resolve useful uncertainty per total cost

DAD turns costly sequential design into a trained history-conditioned policy; iDAD extends this approach to differentiable implicit simulators using a critic. Their appeal is amortization, and their principal cost is the up-front simulated training and policy/critic optimization. For a small finite action/hypothesis space, an explicit information-gain calculation is a simpler initial reference. [DAD](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/foster21a/foster21a.pdf), [iDAD](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/file/d811406316b669ad3d370d78b51b1d2e-Paper.pdf).

ABCI adds a useful distinction: choose experiments informative for the downstream causal query, rather than necessarily reconstructing the entire graph. Its instantiation assumes a causally sufficient additive-noise model class; interventional utility does not remove those assumptions. [Active Bayesian Causal Inference](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/file/675e371eeeea99551ce47797ed6ed33e-Paper-Conference.pdf).

**G — PROPOSED:** compare random, space-filling, disagreement, exact small-grid information gain, fixed cost-aware design and a learned investigator with a shared update rule and candidate grid. Add an action that produces pure nuisance noise: it is surprising, but gives no information about the query. This separates useful information from raw unpredictability. If using approximate information objectives, assess actual held-out predictive/query improvement rather than only the trained acquisition score.

World-model imagination is a concrete precedent for using learned dynamics to train decisions. Dreamer's result motivates testing the value of model rollouts; it does not license treating generated trajectories as observed evidence. [Dreamer](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08744-2).

**G — PROPOSED:** use the same small learned dynamics for ordinary predictions and hypothetical branches, with factual parent, assumptions, action, model version, uncertainty and depth retained. Compare no rollout, short rollout and equal-compute alternatives. A model-generated label does not increment the observed-evidence counter.

## 5. Consolidation requires a validity domain and invalidation path

DreamCoder is the closest reviewed precedent for jointly improving a reusable program library and a learned search guide. Its primitive language, interpreter and wake/sleep procedure are supplied; its learned abstractions and learned guide are distinct outputs. This separation directly motivates K versus eta controls. [DreamCoder PLDI 2021](https://people.csail.mit.edu/asolar/papers/EllisWNSMHCST21.pdf).

Finite program sketching provides a bounded verification precedent: synthesize an implementation against an explicit reference and seek counterexamples. The claimed domain, specification and solver assumptions determine the resulting guarantee. Finite random tests cannot establish equality over an infinite or unspecified domain. [Combinatorial Sketching](https://people.csail.mit.edu/asolar/papers/Solar-LezamaTBSS06.pdf).

**G — PROPOSED:** retain (interface, program, guard, dependency versions, evidence, unresolved assumptions, counterexamples, cost). The deterministic obligation is g(x) => P(x)=F(x). Use exhaustive finite-domain verification first where practical. For stochastic tasks use a declared risk/coverage requirement. Compare supplied guards, learned guards, no guard and dependency invalidation. False guard acceptance is separate from unnecessary rejection.

No reviewed library-learning paper establishes live-state migration for this SERA/Kavi integration. That remains an architectural obligation: changes to a generator, latent basis or primitive invalidate the old interpretation of z unless a checked map or replay preserves its meaning. This missing mechanism should stay visible rather than being hidden behind version IDs.

## 6. Modern recurrent memory is a candidate route, not the whole knowledge system

Gated DeltaNet combines decay with targeted residual writes. DeltaProduct adds multiple transition factors per token. Mamba-3 combines a different discretization, rotations and MIMO computation. These papers motivate concrete write/update and state-tracking ablations, while preserving the finite-memory/cost boundary. A larger or faster recurrent state alone does not add a class posterior, provenance, guards or executable knowledge revision. [Gated DeltaNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06464), [DeltaProduct v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.10297v4), [Mamba-3](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.15569).

Titans makes the online learner explicit inside a neural associative memory, using gradient writes with momentum and decay. Whether such an update becomes persistent knowledge across episodes/restarts depends on the host system; it must be tested separately from context retention. Surprise-sensitive memory also needs the nuisance-noise control. [Titans](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.00663).

KDN is unusually relevant to the proposed uncertainty requirement. It retains a matrix association estimate and confidence state; under its conditional linear-Gaussian assumptions, a Kalman gain weights a residual write according to prior uncertainty and observation noise. Exact covariance is dense. Its diagonal and isotropic approximations reduce *auxiliary covariance* to O(d_k) and O(1) per head, while the full O(d_k*d_v) associative memory remains. This distinction is explicit in both the paper and official README. [KDN](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07816), [official implementation](https://github.com/ngocbh/kalman-delta-networks).

**G — PROPOSED:** before adding a KDN layer, test exact ordinary Kalman/RLS, fixed-gain delta, token-only gates, diagonal confidence and scalar confidence on identical small noisy key-value streams. Vary repeated evidence, key correlation, reliability and abrupt drift. Construct histories with different confidence followed by the same incoming observation. Measure retained-map error, held-out queries, calibration, overwrite interference and total state/update cost. Only then test learned keys/gates. A calibrated memory estimate under one linear model is still not uncertainty about whether that model class is correct.

The newest mechanisms have not been reproduced here. Kernel availability and benchmark quality are not sufficient evidence for integrating them into a persistent system.

## 7. Plasticity and improved learning procedure require separate measurements

Loss of plasticity is the failure to acquire a fresh task after prolonged training; catastrophic forgetting is degraded performance on still-valid old tasks. Dohare et al. provide direct negative evidence that these are distinct and that standard training can lose plasticity in the tested streams. Their unit-replacement mechanism is a candidate, not a license to assume replacing units preserves old knowledge. [Loss of plasticity](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07711-7).

**G — PROPOSED:** measure fresh-task learning curves after increasing stream lengths, old-valid-task retention, correction of outdated assertions, replay bytes and update cost. Compare fresh initialization, replay, regularization/local adapters and any replacement mechanism. Correctly rejecting an old false belief must not be penalized as forgetting.

MAML demonstrates training an initialization for easier adaptation; that initialization can itself contain prior knowledge. Learned-optimizer research shows why update-count gains can disappear after memory/compute overhead and task generalization are considered. Neither result identifies a new eta merely because later combined performance improves. [MAML](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/finn17a/finn17a.pdf), [learned-optimizer tradeoffs](https://arxiv.org/html/2203.11860v3).

**G — PROPOSED:** preserve four cells: (K_old,eta_old), (K_old,eta_new), (K_new,eta_old), (K_new,eta_new). Use paired unseen learning problems, matched initial evidence and matched total cost. Report the eta effect at each K and their interaction. Strong fixed policies, random policy, tuned ordinary learning and equal-compute search remain controls. Record a policy's own training and the amortization break-even point.

For successive generations, predefine fresh future-family panels. Repeatedly inspecting the same sealed test family turns it into validation data. One successful learned-policy update establishes at most a local learning-to-learn effect. No reviewed source establishes unbounded autonomous recursive self-improvement, and the supplied meta-updater V should remain labelled as supplied.

## Implementation status and remaining gaps

This literature subtask changed only files in `02_literature`. It executed searches, source fetches and document/ledger construction. It did not train a model, run a paper's experiments, install an external architecture or inspect source code beyond six official README excerpts. Actual SERA/Kavi code, reproduction and new experiment statuses belong to the main continuation's evidence map and registry.

This is a substantial focused review for the first architecture decision, not exhaustive coverage of every research area in the handoff. In particular, no primary quantum-instrument/tensor-network advantage study, JEPA transfer study, open-ended evolutionary system or successful multi-generation recursive-improvement experiment was reviewed here. Those are open literature workstreams if an executable discriminating hypothesis later requires them.

The next justified local protocol is GG-P0, with the full A–H and ten-family coverage checklist retained. Positive evidence would be a repeatable predictive/retained-cost gain on structured tasks with calibrated failure and repair on exceptions. The essential negative outcome is that arbitrary independent labels do not acquire unseen predictive information, and their exact retained information still costs storage. Any architectural promotion should follow that evidence.
