# Open research gaps

The highest-value unresolved question is whether compact generators can retain useful distinctions and recognize when their supplied class fails. A finite circle fit alone cannot establish that a circle generated the observations.

1. **Representation uncertainty:** the packet's coefficient covariance omitted alternative mechanisms; nominal 95% coverage fell to about 21% under omitted sine structure. Test finite alternatives plus an explicit inadequacy alarm.
2. **Applicability:** existing SERA correction scopes and packet envelopes are supplied. Learn from positive and counterexample evidence, test boundary errors, and keep unresolved cases visible.
3. **Shared transfer:** separate successful probes are not a unified learner. Pin all configuration and numerical ownership, then test whether acquired mechanisms improve another route under matched exposure.
4. **Acquisition:** information gain helps the packet's in-class world, but no reliable learned-policy improvement was established. Retain the fixed investigator as the comparison.
5. **State and revision:** preserve admitted observations across version changes, reject stale dependencies, and distinguish identity-schema replay from learned latent migration.
6. **Grounding and access:** supplied phase/coordinates, known noise and reset privileges are inductive bias. Unordered geometry, partial observation, reachable control and language remain separate stages.
7. **Compression:** measure entire serialized models, decoders, guards, witnesses and discovery work. Random independent facts must remain an explicit negative control.
8. **Recursive improvement:** actual K/eta generations and crossed evaluations are required. Loop execution or a favorable combined score does not establish M3 or M4.

The first executable geometry cohort covers circles, ellipses, localized exceptions and random values. Periodic functions, spirals, piecewise curves, hierarchy, noncommuting transforms, dynamics and unordered point sets remain on the full-suite checklist.
