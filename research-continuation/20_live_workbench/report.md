# A usable persistent learner and a rejected learned procedure

I connected the actual saved R1 owner to a local application for task-time numerical acquisition, changing-data analysis, continuing control and verified finite equations. The application is running locally and uses saved learned coefficients for execution. New data changes those registered coefficients, while the archive retains earlier observations, fitted states, source identities and failed attempts.

## What works

Task-time fitting retrieves a labeled local material record or accepts new paired examples. It fits an affine or quadratic rule, selects using a validation split, checks a separate withheld split, and executes accepted inputs through the actual R1 owner. A browser request learned Celsius-to-Fahrenheit conversion and returned 0→32, 20→68, 37→98.6 and 100→212. The four withheld examples had maximum error 5.68e-14. Those examples derive from a published unit rule, not independent physical observations.

The fresh ONDEMAND-001 qualification evaluated 90 numerical tasks: 60 exact polynomial tasks accepted, 30 omitted-sinusoid tasks withheld. All 540 independently evaluated accepted queries were accurate to 6.76e-14 or better. All 90 out-of-domain probes were withheld. These results validate supplied-family fitting and guards; they do not establish arbitrary function learning or general mathematical expertise.

The live inbox automatically processed a 24-observation file, detected an eight-row append, and saved a second 32-observation version and new reports. The browser also processed a separate 48-row synthetic stream: causal MAE 0.0786 versus persistence MAE 0.0968. That single demonstrator is a usability check, not a prospective population claim. Forecast uncertainty remains uncalibrated, and surprise flags do not identify causes.

The continuing control session progressed from 79 to 85 observations and 84 actions. Its finite equation skill still returned x=0 for 4*x−5=6 modulo 11 afterward. Session restoration, CSV corrections, source migration, predecessor preservation and duplicate-request protection are covered by tests. All original neural tensors remain unchanged.

## Learned eta: qualified comparison, rejected promotion

A09-PILOT-001 trained eight meta-regression coefficients on 576 supplied numerical observations. Two development lifetimes were preserved. A09-FINAL-001 reused that trained state without retraining and evaluated ten new independent lifetimes with three successive generations each. Successors inherit their full earlier K and updated eta; the final arms separately cross old/new starting K and old/new eta. Equal observed values are available, with the old-K ablation limited to its last eight earlier values.

| Future prediction policy, full K | Mean squared error |
|---|---:|
| Fixed observed-error selector | 0.140200 |
| Learned update selector | 0.102244 |
| Fixed AR(2), window 16 | 0.138950 |
| Persistence | 0.102122 |

The learned selector improves 27.07% over the observed-error selector, with paired lifetime absolute-gain 95% interval [0.014955, 0.061977]. It improves that comparison in each generation on average, but fails the strongest-control gate: persistence is slightly better. It is **rejected** as an application default and does not establish independent investigator superiority. The selected methods include persistence for 618 of 720 full-K learned-eta decisions. More stored K does not independently demonstrate positive transfer.

The independent audit reconstructs the meta-regression through a separate least-squares fit, checks 4,320 reported prediction errors and verifies successive K/eta continuity. It does not independently reimplement every AR fit. All costs and negative outcomes remain preserved. No parameters were tuned against final outcomes.

## Verification and limits

All 233 collected test cases completed successfully across the preserved runs: an 80-second full-suite job completed the first 202 pass indicators before timing out, then the remaining 31 passed in a dedicated job. The 13 application/task tests also passed together earlier. The timeout remains a failed resource-limited run, not a clean full-suite pass. A new 61-group retention bank reproduces both group and individual-case scores exactly, using the actual application owner with three acquired contexts and 85 interaction observations.

The source architecture is preserved as one registered owner with explicit state migration and proof rebinding. New numerical contexts are 40-byte float64 records. They are supplied statistical/procedural routes, not evidence of learned shared representation or universal task understanding. General language acquisition, paper comprehension, autonomous curriculum construction and general theorem proving remain open. The user now permits forgetting when reacquisition is demonstrated; preserving the old neural route in this release does not establish that larger reacquisition claim.
