# ST-001 — textbook relationships on the continuing learner

## Frozen purpose and method

I extend the actual Stage 30 GroundR1 owner with learned sparse quantitative relationships and extend its study interface with a bounded acquire/check/update/retry loop. The curriculum uses human-authored University Physics worked calculations and equations from the pinned source in RECONCILIATION.md. It covers momentum, impulse, Newton's second law, kinetic energy, parallel work, average power, spring energy, gravitational energy, Ohm's law, electric power, transported charge and sensible heat.

The teacher supplies named quantities, dimensions, assumptions and transcribed source equations. The learner receives source examples, enumerates a finite monomial dictionary (integer powers -2 through 2), uses dimensional compatibility to filter candidates, and fits the remaining coefficients by least squares. The independent source-equation checker controls admission. Its reference coefficients are withheld from the fitter. Source arithmetic is retained exactly before the textbook's printed rounding. No generated example is used to train these relationships. This is supervised quantitative acquisition with supplied representations.

Weights and fitting sufficient statistics reside in registered modules on the existing owner. Each successful acquisition changes its identity and rebinds the guarded library. Earlier tensors stay byte-identical. Requests execute learned coefficients; disabling them must prevent successful use. Source records supplement those weights with provenance and applicability.
