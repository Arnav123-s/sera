# Primary research and local deductions

I used a focused primary-source lookup to inform the discriminating mechanism, then tested the local implementation.

- [DreamCoder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381) connects executable library learning with neural recognition trained from replay and conditional generated tasks. It motivates the knowledge-to-neural-learning path. Our circle family is supplied; this experiment does not reproduce its library-learning system or justify its broader results for SERA.
- [Learning without Forgetting](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.09282), Li and Hoiem, constrains original outputs while adapting a network. It supports investigating parent-output preservation. Here I additionally retain old replay and use support inputs from several modalities. This is an adaptation of a general idea, not a claim of a new retention algorithm or that the model invented the procedure.

Five search results and two fetched primary URLs were examined across this continuation. The attempted `1703.04200` fetch returned no useful body and is not used as evidence. No literature-derived performance number substitutes for a local result.

The decisive local deduction is algebraic: the chosen circle normalization cancels the learned coefficients. The independent simulator control and a coefficient-intervention check are therefore necessary to distinguish general conditional teaching from acquired-instance transfer. This restriction is carried into the architecture audit and final gate interpretation.

The confirmatory uncertainty unit is a teaching/optimizer stream with a new paired assessment bank. Five streams share the same pretrained parent. The saved interval is a descriptive paired bootstrap over those five stream means, at 97.5% to reflect two frozen candidate scopes. It is neither a proof of lifelong transfer nor a finite-sample conditional-risk certificate.
