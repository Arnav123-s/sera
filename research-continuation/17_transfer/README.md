## Research question and limits

Can conditional samples from the acquired circle candidate improve the existing neural motion route on new circle trajectories without losing old capabilities? The learned route receives three coordinate observations and predicts the next point. It has no phase, center, velocity or generator access at evaluation.

The circle grammar, similarity transforms and constant angular speed are supplied. Normalizing the acquired circle removes its instance-specific center/radius. This tests reuse within an assumed family, not discovery of that family, and cannot by itself show that acquired coefficients contain transferable structural knowledge. Conditional teaching records retain their model-target provenance and never become independent observations. The existing GG-GUARD-002 certificate is not applied to this different policy.

I compare observed-only learning, detached conditional learning, integrated shared learning, an extra-owner observed-only control and an independent-simulator exposure control. Every arm has the same optimizer starts, update counts, target/replay batch shapes and old replay access. Unique labels, conditional labels, extra model bytes, wall time and full study costs are reported separately. Equal update counts are not a claim of exactly equal machine computation.

[DreamCoder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381) motivates testing whether executable knowledge can supply useful conditional training tasks for a neural route. Its learned program library and recognition policy are substantially broader than this supplied circle grammar. The result here is established by the actual local controls, not inferred from that paper. [Research notes](research-notes.md) record the primary sources and their limits.
