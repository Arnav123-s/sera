## The coefficient cancellation test

The source circle is

`f_theta(t) = c + R(pi*t) (a,b)`.

Let `r = sqrt(a*a+b*b)` and `phi = atan2(b,a)`. The teaching transform uses

`(f_theta((angle-phi)/pi) - c) / r`.

For any nonzero radius this simplifies exactly to `(cos(angle), sin(angle))`. The subsequent sampled translation and scale determine the teaching circle. Therefore the transformed teaching distribution is independent of every fitted coefficient of the original instance.

This was identified before confirmatory results were interpreted. It is a substantive restriction: even a favorable neural-transfer score cannot establish causal transfer of the acquired numerical instance. It can establish a narrower benefit of conditional teaching from a supplied reusable circle family. A future acquisition-dependent experiment must preserve an instance-specific quantity in the target or withhold the rule itself, include an old/unacquired-K intervention, and verify that the intervention changes the teaching distribution before spending a confirmatory training budget.

## Why both update scopes are retained

The first development candidate improved motion and forgot old outputs. The second added support-only parent-output constraints and still exceeded the retention margin. The third protected all existing representations and adapted only the numerical readout. Those pilots use different development streams, so their metric differences are diagnostic rather than paired causal estimates.

The final two candidates were frozen together before their first outcomes. They share future streams and differ only in trainable scope: 478,483 shared-path parameters versus the 514 existing motion-readout parameters. The latter is useful as a retention control, but cannot demonstrate improved shared representations because those representations are held fixed.

The extra-capacity control duplicates an entire owner while keeping the same update scope and examples. It isolates the cost of a separate model. It is not an exhaustive comparison with larger trainable architectures, ensembles or extra independently observed data. The simulator-exposure control accounts for the latter data source explicitly.

No final result will remove the coefficient-cancellation limitation. No final result alone will establish useful imagination, online investigation, hidden-state migration, sustained plasticity, or an improved learning procedure. A08/A09 require their own continuing-state and future-task interventions.

## Completed follow-up and final source comparison

The raw-coordinate follow-up preserved the actual acquired coefficients. Its preflight intervened on the original 16-observation K and the corrected K with one independently acquired recheck, while holding their initial neural weights equal. Five fresh streams and four controls were frozen before outcomes. Corrected teaching reduced ordinary MSE by 14.97% against observed-only learning and 23.26% against incorrect-K teaching. Both gains were positive in all five streams; none of the 61 retained groups lost measured score. Faster-turning generalization remains weak. The supplied law and one simulated physical instance restrict this result to acquired-instance readout transfer.

This supplies a narrow A06 contribution: a different neural route benefits, without executing the generator at prediction. It does not establish a unique benefit of ownership: the normalized detached/integrated states are identical by construction. The 514-parameter readout control protects shared representations rather than improving them. The larger 478,483-parameter update lost 4.688 percentage points in its worst retained group and is rejected. That unresolved plasticity requirement stays on the checklist.

The predeclared qualifying descendant was integrated into one experimental solver. All 16 factual observations and the correction were replayed, original cumulative costs were carried forward, both stale bindings were rejected, finite programs were fully re-proved, generator outputs stayed bitwise equal, and the entire solver reloaded exactly. The original operational store and interpreter sources remain unchanged. This advances A07 dependency handling and the factual-replay baseline described in A08; it does not complete A08's intervention-sensitive decision or hidden-state gate. No statistical certificate was moved to the new owner.
