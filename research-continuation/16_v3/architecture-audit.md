## Decisions against the packet's failure rules

GC-001 fails the full-work test and remains a failed candidate. GC-002 changes the proof implementation without discarding proof cases. Review then exposed a restricted-domain assumption and missing actual-interpreter enforcement. Their source versions, failed regression, corrected checks and migrated successors remain separate artifacts. The studies' finite forward relations satisfy the new domain condition; no final outcomes or thresholds were tuned during the repair.

GG-GUARD-002 passes its prespecified component gate but does not solve the hardest mechanisms. There is no demonstrated superiority of the grouped policy over the simpler global threshold; the latter has higher utility in the reported cohort. Keeping both conditional results is more accurate than presenting conservatism as a universal performance improvement.

W08/A06 still requires a distinct learned route to benefit from acquired knowledge under no-program, detached, integrated and capacity controls, with retained old capabilities. W09/A09 still requires continuing adaptation and a separately evaluated eta. This release does not claim those stages or M2–M4.
