# Architecture audit against the packets and original direction

The destination remains one continuing learner with learned reusable computation,
conditional imagination, grounded correction and eventual improvement of the
learning procedure. This release qualifies a narrow connection, not the entire
architecture.

| Contract | Implemented evidence | Remaining limit |

The new actual learner acquired a finite role interface, a proposal mapping trained
on parent-generated conditional examples, and measured landmark position statistics.
I supplied the grammar, entity vocabulary, goal meanings, conditional data generator,
analytic teacher, optimizer, observation adapter, scheduler, persistence and tests.
Sharing those components does not by itself mean that SERA invented them.

The original handbook's separate state-time, weight-update and eta-update contracts
remain visible. Latent refinement changes branch state. Adam trained new parameters.
Location and motion observations update numerical sufficient statistics. Eta stays
fixed. Compact generators represent structured families with assumptions and
exceptions; nothing here implements infinite arbitrary memory.

The original R1 core and all predecessor experiment implementations are untouched.
The new 11,336 parameters include both language and proposal interfaces; they are
additional to the existing learner and history. Full storage is listed separately
in the local-state manifest. No claim uses only a small state vector as the cost of
the entire system. No quantum operator is promoted on mathematical validity alone.

The scope repair is explicit: after the final surface-composition failure, I narrowed
runtime admission to the previously taught active/passive productions. I did not
retrain on those final sentences, delete their predictions, change the scientific
scores or call the narrower admission rule a fresh generalization success.
