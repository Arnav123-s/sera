## Finite prospective experiment

Final has twelve worlds per family (seeds 46101 through 46112). Teacher coefficients, measurement noise and query generation use separate deterministic seeds. Existing v16 final worlds are not used to set this experiment's decisions.

For each world, start with 12 narrow-direction observations. Acquire 12 more from 128 candidate states by either model disagreement plus design variance or a balanced geometric allocation. Commit the selected query before exposing its response. Use 16 separate observations to choose the representation and 32 further observations to check adequacy. Total exposure is 72 observed acceleration pairs per procedure. No final label participates in fitting, selection, confidence or rewards.

The original request is retained before acquisition: predict acceleration at `[qx,qy,vx,vy,ux,uy]=[0.3,0.7,-0.1,0.2,0.1,0]`. Save its initial radial answer after the first twelve observations, return to the identical state after investigation, and independently assess both only after fitting and qualification. That point is an additional held-out goal check, separate from the random final bank.

Controls: frozen initial radial; radial fit using all 72 pairs (extra-data control); always-directional fit on the 24 acquisition pairs; balanced acquisition with qualification; disagreement acquisition with qualification; actual current R1 features with a ridge readout. All acquired, selection and qualification labels are charged. Fit an independent duplicate of each selected structured model and compare predictions. Retain every control and rejected candidate.

Selection requires both a 20% relative and 0.0001 absolute selection-MSE improvement before relaxing symmetry. Independent adequacy requires RMSE at most 0.04 and a 95th-percentile absolute residual at most 0.09, plus finite, passive fitted parameters. The adequacy bank is not added to model fitting. A failed adequacy check leaves the original question open with the competing conditional predictions and a request for a new mechanism. This is an engineered finite gate, not a learned universal investigator or calibrated proof of completeness.

Final evaluation: 256 fresh states per world, including unseen directions; 64 wider-range states reported separately; four held-out trajectory initial conditions; and one goal-conditioned finite control comparison. Acceleration MSE has units m^2/s^4; report position and velocity trajectory RMSE separately in m and m/s. Freeze before opening these banks. Primary integration gate: qualified directional worlds improve held-out error over their initial radial model; radial worlds retain the simpler route; omitted mechanisms are rejected by adequacy.
