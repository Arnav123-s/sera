# Development decision before final evaluation

TT-PILOT-001 completed four independent task-family seeds, 648 fits/policy outcomes. Its independent audit passed. At 12 fitting examples, acquired structure recovered all four related tasks and two of four sparse-innovation tasks; scratch recovered neither family. The paired mean reduction in future tolerance violations was 0.6621, with the prespecified approximate 95% lower limit 0.4446. This is development evidence only.

The stronger full-label-cost control recovered all four related, innovation and dense tasks. Its additional current-task examples account for amortized prior acquisition cost. I will not claim that reuse reduces lifetime observation cost from this result. Reuse matters when old knowledge is already available and new-task labels are scarce.

Three accepted local-exception tasks missed deliberately targeted exception probes. Their iid future error rates still lay below the stated 5% risk target. Therefore a distributional gate cannot authorize pointwise verified answers. The runtime will expose empirical estimates, explicit tolerance/assumptions, no extrapolation, preserved provenance and withdrawal on contradictory new observations. It will not put these answers through the existing formal-answer badge.

The acquisition algorithm and candidate grids remain unchanged. The final cohort is TT-FINAL-001: 24 independent task-family seeds beginning 730003 at stride 1009, disjoint from development. The frozen study protocol defines 6 target families, 3 fitting counts and 9 policies, totaling 3,888 outcomes. The final will be read once and audited without refitting. A positive final transfer condition permits only the optional empirical numerical route, contingent on actual-owner preservation, persistence, guard and drift tests. Full implementation, exact costs and original-architecture comparison remain required.

No numerical failures were discarded. Candidate LP infeasibility for out-of-family data is retained in candidate traces and falls back to the existing selection policy. The source basis is acquired by SVD; the supplied polynomial vocabulary and externally written learner are not learned eta.
