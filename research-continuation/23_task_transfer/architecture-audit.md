## Integration decision

I retain and expose the bounded numerical route through `scripts/sera_tasks.py`, using an explicitly preserved descendant rather than silently migrating the running workbench. The positive task-structure result, abstention behavior, exact restart, original-route retention and calibration-scope repair support this use. It requires normalized numerical CSV examples and reports empirical predictions. It is not added to the formal-answer route and is not presented as a general task learner or a new investigator policy.

The extension adds owned task weights and accumulated K. It does not establish that the recurrent learner has independently become a better learner. Learned dictionary/feature discovery beyond the supplied polynomial vocabulary, externally grounded prerequisite acquisition, and robust informative investigation remain the next larger uncertainties.
