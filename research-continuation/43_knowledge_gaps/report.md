# Self-identified gaps and competing explanations: KG-001

SERA selected a gap from its own retained observation record, generated **12,288 distinct candidate programs**, and retained **eight qualified empirical explanations** on the continuing owner. The selected program predicted 13 reserved positions with **0.026596 m RMSE**. All earlier 380 tensor records remained unchanged.

## What initiated the investigation

The owner contained 149 retained equation/association records and one attributed observation record. Its existing source metadata recorded 93 measurements, of which 20 had been used in an earlier interval-specific study. A generic evidence-coverage rule selected that record. It was not given a target force, a missing-mechanism label or a completed equation.

The learner acquired the remaining rows from the source bytes it already possessed. Its retained constant-acceleration explanation differed from the newly examined development positions by 20.4784 m RMSE when extended beyond its previous interval. This formed an explicit coverage-gap question. The old interval and its valid simple model remain preserved; this result diagnoses that extrapolation, rather than invalidating the earlier in-scope result.

The source is the earlier [Madison College laboratory trajectory](https://madisoncollegephysics.net/comp/data_freefall.csv), SHA-256 `8e33086344445868dee132927f9cbb83c91285cd4d16a4ae912fe4f518296c7e`. No new source, teacher example or corpus was added. All 20 previously consumed rows were excluded from new scores. The remaining 73 rows were separated by fixed source/row hashes into **47 development, 13 credit and 13 final rows**. These are reserved observations from one retained trajectory, not 73 independent experiments.

## How the explanations were constructed

SERA executed its retained learned integral weights repeatedly to obtain polynomial basis functions. Search fitted their coefficients and explored generic conditional programs: separate polynomial intervals, continuous joins and joins preserving the first derivative. Boundary locations, degrees and coefficients came from development evidence. There was no supplied bounce, drag, launch or hidden-force answer template.

The engineering contribution is the inventory reader, fixed coverage priority, finite program grammar, search operators, cross-validation, independent assessor and reward accounting. The learned outputs are the fitted executable coefficients and, in the learned comparison, proposal-ranking weights. The selected residual-guided procedure is fixed. Its selection does not establish improved learning-to-learn.

One frozen study compared three procedures, with 4,096 distinct programs each:

| Procedure | Proposed programs | Fitted/checked programs | Duplicate proposals rejected | First selected candidate final RMSE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Learned proposal ranking | 4,096 | 2,365 | 8,079 | 0.035444 m |
| Residual-guided search, selected before final | 4,096 | 2,785 | 5,616 | 0.026596 m |
| Balanced exploration | 4,096 | 2,591 | 1,480 | 0.024934 m |

The selected procedure was fixed by development loss before credit or final access. Balanced exploration scored better on this final; the choice was not changed afterward. Search attempted 27,463 proposals in total, including duplicates, and kept all 12,288 distinct program records, including **4,547 rejected fits**. Repeated development checks guide search; they are not counted as new factual discoveries.

## The missing influence expressed mathematically

The interface differentiates each learned position program and compares the resulting acceleration with the previously retained value, -9.653059 m/s². At source time 3.5 s, the selected program predicts acceleration -0.004398 m/s², corresponding to an additional **+9.648661 m/s²** relative to that extrapolated explanation. This is a model-conditional residual derived from the learner's own fitted weights. The cause remains an explicit investigation goal requiring an independently observed explanatory variable.

This calculation makes the missing quantity executable: each alternative states different consequences, rather than merely attaching a word such as “unknown force.” The query interface also selects a coordinate where the surviving explanations disagree most. It records the proposed check without presenting a simulated outcome as a measurement.
