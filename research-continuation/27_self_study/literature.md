# Sources, alternatives and interpretation

I used the v11 packet as a research addendum and independently replayed its finite
evidence. Its source-provided coefficient representation, fixed selectors and
automated checker remain explicit. Goal-filtered random reading is the initial
control because v11's chosen information-gain method spent its tight budget on
informative-looking invalid claims. Retained knowledge and a better selector are
separate experimental questions.

Donald Knuth's [Johann Faulhaber and sums of powers](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9207222v1)
provides a concrete source-to-capability task. The versioned HTML exposes the
paper's power-sum equations as mathematical annotations. I implemented a bounded
reader for that representation and independently check every imported equation.
This is supplied mathematical parsing; the coefficients of SERA's retained
operator are learned from admitted examples. A closed-form symbolic solver is
the strong installed reference. Merely reading/caching the paper is a separate
control. The arXiv source remains unchanged when a claim is rejected.

OpenStax [Calculus Volume 1, section4.10](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/4-10-antiderivatives)
connects antiderivatives, polynomial power rules and one-dimensional initial-value
motion. I supplied that schema to generate a small independent teaching curriculum.
SERA then learns the numerical operator and composes it twice for acceleration,
velocity and position. The physical assumptions and initial conditions are inputs;
correct polynomial execution is checked separately from validity in a real system.

[MASSIVE](https://huggingface.co/datasets/AmazonScience/massive), published by
Amazon and licensed CC BY4.0, supplies translated task requests. The original
publisher archive was streamed without running dataset scripts. Each locale has
its own text and labels, but parallel semantic IDs overlap across languages.
Consequently the experiment measures acquiring new-language mappings on existing
request categories. It does not treat translated versions as independent new
semantic tasks. Replay and uninterrupted optimizer state are engineering inputs.

The official [Clay statement by Bombieri](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/riemann.pdf)
fixes the RH parent goal in terms of nontrivial zeros of the meromorphically
continued zeta function. Polynomial integration is a useful prerequisite for
finite logarithmic-series calculations. Extending it to a zero-free Euler-product
argument requires independently justified convergence, logarithm identities and
rearrangements. The parent statement remains fixed as these obligations are studied.

[arXiv's API manual](https://info.arxiv.org/help/api/user-manual.html) and
[terms](https://info.arxiv.org/help/api/tou.html) guide source access. The adapter
uses HTTPS, one request at a time, at least3.1seconds between requests, bounded
responses, a research user agent, exact versioned URLs and checksummed caching.
Paper text remains data. Public artifacts contain source receipts and derived
mathematical examples; original article bodies remain in the local cache.
