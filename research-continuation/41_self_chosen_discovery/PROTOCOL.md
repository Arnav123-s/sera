# SD-001: examples, self-chosen questions, conjectures and independent credit

This is a prospective protocol. The current admitted parent is Stage 38 in `runs/sera-growth-live`; Stages 39 and 40 remain preserved experimental successors. Their completed final cohorts will not select or tune this experiment.

## Requested behavior

The learner selects a question from gaps in its retained executable knowledge, generates competing explanations from its own executions, imagines consequences, commits the candidates before external assessment, checks assumptions, retains useful scoped discoveries and learns which investigations produced verified progress. Three elementary worked examples teach the process first. A separate transfer check assesses use of that process. Examples are supplied teaching, not autonomous discoveries.

The examples distinguish (1) a conjecture supported by new checks, (2) a genuinely different successful route versus a renamed copy, and (3) a plausible generalization corrected by a counterexample. Their toy arithmetic is explicitly an illustration. They do not contain target equations or target language associations for the research phase.

## One owner and broad starting inventory

Continue the actual `GrowingR1`, its learned integral/sum operators, learned request interpretation, conditional motion route, retained discoveries and source/applicability gates. Add owned procedure weights and independently admitted executable relationship weights. Preserve inherited weights exactly during this study, so each discovery is an addition rather than an untested replacement.

Self-generated investigations can concern operator compositions, constant and changing motion, or regularities in retained human language requests. Question generation reads the actual available primitives and owner predictions. Search expressions and sparse fitting are engineered tools; the questions chosen, fitted coefficients, proposed relationships, counterexamples, learned policy updates and retained rules are experimental outcomes. Supplied arithmetic, units, variable names and search operations are not independently invented concepts.

## Experiment and boundaries

Freeze executable source identities, three teaching examples, finite search limits, source partitions, seeds, comparisons and decision thresholds before the main run. Use a learned investigator and a balanced control with the same candidate, verification and practice budgets. Keep bounded exploration and count every attempted candidate, including rejects. Run a sustained sequence of investigations, checkpointing choices, random state, weights, unfinished questions and the complete credit ledger. Stop early only for exhausted distinct questions or an owned resource limit, recording the exact frontier.

Deduplicate canonical expressions and already retained consequences. Award distinct-route credit only when an alternative adds independently verified execution coverage, a different valid assumption set or a measured computational saving. Algebraic restatements and more words do not earn new-discovery points. Credit for an accurate new connection, extra verified scope, or a cheaper successful procedure is separately itemized and bounded. Unchecked imagination remains conditional. Neither novelty nor accumulated points can grant factual status, permissions or applicability.

Candidate proposals and their predictions are committed before their verification evidence is opened. Exact mathematics requires a separate coefficient proof checker and fresh execution replay. Empirical language associations require human annotations from groups withheld from candidate fitting, and are reported as scoped associations. Real physical observations are acquired only after relevant candidate predictions have been committed; retain original sources, units, measurement uncertainty and all residuals. A model identity or simulated trajectory is not an observation.

Development checks may guide continuing investigation, with total attempts counted. A reserved final is opened once after selection. Do not train on its outcome or switch to another candidate after seeing it. Compare the learned investigator with balanced exploration at matched cost; any claim of better learning procedures requires future-task comparisons with knowledge and procedure changes separated.

## Required evidence

- Three teaching examples, learning updates, and a separate process-transfer test.
- The learner's chosen questions, why they were open in its prior registry, complete generated candidates, imagined consequences and pre-check hashes.
- Independent accept/reject receipts, counterexamples, scoped assumptions and exact non-duplicated credit.
- Actual owned retained weights, restart equivalence, inherited capability retention, and unchanged source/applicability gates.
- A readable account of discoveries, alternative routes, empirical checks and remaining open questions.
- Full local costs, including interruptions, failed candidates and archive/test work.

The user most recently asked for a few elementary examples followed by broad self-directed discovery and extra credit for distinct accurate ideas. Novelty is measured against SERA's retained registry; scientific priority relative to human literature is a separate claim requiring separate evidence.
