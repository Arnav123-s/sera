# Development decisions before new final cohorts

The user expanded the study to memory, weight learning and multiple uses of sparse constraints, and asked me to read Tao's own explanations and MRI work. These are separate research arms, not changes to the live learner's architecture.

The first application pilot used seed 710011: 244 completed reconstructions/adaptations. Independent equations and all available LP certificates passed. A 32-value sketch recovered a four-entry vector, but required 272 numerical/metadata bytes versus 56 for direct sparse storage. Sparse-error coding repaired four corruptions in 64 received values; 24 corruptions failed. Sparse output-weight adaptation worked in a fixed tanh network while a dense update was not recovered. Algorithms and thresholds remain unchanged for a 24-seed final, starting at 910021.

The first imaging pilot used seed 810013: 192 reconstructions. The arithmetic audit passed, but pixel reconstruction of a sparse image from 128 random Fourier samples retained NRMSE 0.802. Its extremely small fixed penalty, combined with only 250 iterations, made slow progress along unmeasured directions. This is a failed optimizer configuration, preserved with its source and outputs. I added five-stage regularization continuation with acceleration restarts and increased the fixed budget to 400 iterations. That change is tested only on a new two-seed development cohort beginning at 820019 before any imaging final is read.

The intended imaging final has 12 fresh seeds starting at 1010027, the same four synthetic families, two sampling counts, four geometries, two noise levels and three methods. In addition to global NRMSE and ROI absolute error, the independent audit measures mean contrast inside the 2x2 faint-feature ROI against its immediately surrounding ring. This diagnostic is declared before the final; the true contrast is approximately 0.04. It does not supply an image or ROI to the decoder. Small global error alone does not establish feature preservation.

Final outcomes will not trigger another hyperparameter sweep on those seeds. All arms are descriptive screening across many conditions, not a claim of statistical significance. Any practical repair utility must add an independent payload digest and reject an incorrect reconstruction; reconstruction residual alone is insufficient. The live owner's supported-answer path requires its own downstream evidence and will not be changed merely because one synthetic arm succeeds.
