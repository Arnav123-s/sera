## Integration decision

The common-dictionary predictor's gate fails: 83/96 adequate cases recovered and 15/98 accepted cases wrong in its target strata. The cost-constrained disagreement policy also fails. Neither enters the live supported-answer route. This follows the v3 requirement to qualify the complete policy, including acquisition and validation, before promotion.

The small-file repair command is an explicit research utility with an independent digest gate. It acts on copied artifact bytes, preserves the source, and returns no output after an integrity failure. It is not automatically invoked by SERA and does not install a new owner. The real-owner readout study is a maintained experimental descendant whose parent is restored after every trial.

The most useful next hypothesis is **sparse innovations in a representation learned from prior tasks**, tested with wrong-prior and dense-change controls and an independently calibrated complete acquisition/answer policy. Another needed step is externally sourced task acquisition beyond the finite teacher, preserving retrieval provenance and independently checking what was learned. Neither gap is filled by naming a classical optimizer a general intelligence system.
