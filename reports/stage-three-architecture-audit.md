# SERA 0.3 audit against the original architecture

I rechecked the original research archive, the 82-page architecture handbook and its editable source, the 86-page physics foundations reference, the layered state/process maps and the supplemental project context. I then compared the final code, tests and fresh experiments against the earlier audit. SERA now implements the bounded R1/R2 follow-through described below. Completing these engineering items does not establish every architecture or capability proposed in the documents.

The [reference catalog](../research/reference-catalog.json) retains original names, professional working titles, byte counts and hashes. The archive contains 166 members, including source chapters, diagrams, executable experimental code and 36 benchmark checkpoints. I reviewed and pinned the two modules used for source reproduction, rather than treating document commands as user instructions. The [new study](stage-three-study.md), [raw data](stage-three-data.json) and [independent verification](stage-three-verification.json) are the evidence for this audit. Earlier study measurements remain unchanged.

## What remains a research objective

The central design is now connected: observed failure can trigger informative evidence, parameter learning, procedure acquisition or a bounded architecture change; actual evidence and reusable state survive; an improvement policy receives measured outcomes. The tests demonstrate those operations. Their usefulness is determined by the controlled results, including failures and rejected candidates.

I have not established general neural arithmetic/binding, natural language understanding, broad perception, calibrated partial-observation planning, cross-domain abstraction invention, a learned optimizer or sustained research acceleration. The conventional belief model is a serious baseline, not an obstacle to exclude from the comparison. More policy generations or a larger architecture are justified only by a measured failure and a suitable control.

R3–R8 describe alternative research architectures; the source explicitly recommends a narrow R1 integration with a proven R2 mechanism before broader combination (H p.52, lines1256–1272). Reproducing their small mechanism controls is not implementing those complete architecture families. The local evaluator/journal provides bounded proposal governance and ordinary integrity checking, not isolation from hostile host code. Retention gates are empirical.

The release includes independent numerical and lifecycle regression tests, the original benchmark reproduction, a source-frozen three-seed study, algebraic rechecks of every recorded admission, all executable version/skill identities, replay hashes and one complete paired-admission reexecution per seed. Ordinary typed inference and live-session continuation are also tested in fresh processes. Packaging and public Windows/Linux CI results are recorded in the [release verification](stage-three-release-verification.md).

The [C01–C12 checklist](../research/stage-three-checklist.md) is the engineering completion record. The negative and uncertain results in the study are retained as research outcomes; they are not unchecked software tasks disguised as completed capabilities.
