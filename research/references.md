# Research references

I use the following working titles and filenames throughout SERA. They describe each document's role without making a claim about achieved intelligence or experimental performance.

| Working title | Working filename | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| SERA Research Archive | `sera-research-archive.zip` | Architecture designs, prototype code and results, diagrams, source graph and finite configuration registry |
| SERA Architecture Handbook | `sera-architecture-handbook.pdf` | 82-page architecture and learning handbook; matching `.md` and `.html` versions are available |
| Quantum Theory Foundations | `quantum-theory-foundations.pdf` | 86-page mathematical reference covering states, dynamics, measurement, composition and computational limits |
| Quantum State and Process Maps | `quantum-state-and-process-maps.pdf` | 14 diagrams connecting representations, state transformations, measurements and approximations |
| SERA Project Context | `sera-project-context.txt` | Supplemental project rationale supplied with the naming update; interpreted in [project context](project-context.md) |

These are SERA's working names for supplied materials. The [reference catalog](reference-catalog.json) maps them to original filenames or archive members and records each complete SHA-256 digest. The [original source manifest](source_manifest.json) and historical experimental evidence retain their source identities.

The local working files live in `research/reference-materials/`. Each is a byte-identical copy of its source; embedded document titles, archive member names, equations and source attribution are preserved. The reference copies remain outside Git history. The catalog, context interpretation and SERA's independent results are committed.

The archive includes its original prototype reports. Their experiments are distinct from SERA's [first study](../reports/first-study.md), [connected study](../reports/connected-study.md) and [architecture audit](../reports/original-source-alignment-audit.md).

The [0.3 source reproduction](../reports/stage-three-source-reproduction.json) separately rechecks the original checkpoints and reruns all 36 benchmark training combinations. The [0.3 results](../reports/stage-three-study.md) remain a separate experiment.
